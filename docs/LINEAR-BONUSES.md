# Linear bonuses (planned, not built)

**Ruling (maintainer, 2026-10-15):** bonuses stack and are not curved out. If something says it
cuts 10%, it cuts 10%. Every bonus screen must show what the bonus actually does.

**Status: deferred.** The curves stay as they are until every location and other bonus source has
been reworked (the location rework is still going). Then the curve system is reworked against the
final numbers, using the audit below as the starting list.

**Decided so far:**

- Clocks read "reduces X's clock by Y%", where 100% would take it to zero.

**Still open, to settle when the bonuses are final:**

- How plain sums are kept from breaking (caps per bonus, smaller sources, or both).
- Whether cuts from separate layers (the Veteran after the muster cut, the Engineer after the build
  cost, the Salvager after the yard) apply one after another or add into one sum.
- Whether fight mechanics (jamming, mending, taunt, intimidation reach, ambush, the pack bonus) and
  the raid disruption penalty keep their curves.

## The audit (2026-10-15)

### How the numbers were taken

- I read location values at level 5 with `bonusesAt` over `EVERY_LOCATION` and the unified bonuses
  with `unifiedBonusFor`. Perks come from `PERK_CATALOG`, research sums from `RESEARCH_ITEMS` (all
  ten rungs of every track) and modification maxima from `MODIFICATIONS` with `fitsIn` and the
  slot levels. I enumerated all of these with scripts against the live source.
- **Realistic** means a strong late crew:
  - its home city's ground, plus the district bonus if its faction holds the district whole;
  - finished research;
  - structures at level 20 with a full deck in the structure the card belongs to;
  - the Overseer's signature perk plus one copy of the best rollable perk;
  - the chair passive at a good grade.
- **Theoretical** means every source in every city, every fitting slot spent on the one effect,
  and one copy of each perk. Rollable perks are distinct within one person but can repeat across
  the 13 chairs, so the perk share of a theoretical sum can be several times higher.
- Can a crew stack sources from different cities? Yes. `standingEffectsFor` folds every location
  in the world that the crew holds, and I found no cap on holdings. Mission boards are home city
  only, so the per-city mission bonuses pay only there.
- Faction cards (Ace, King, Queen, Jack, Joker) feed no curved channel. They are all linear today.
- The pattern behind most BREAKS rows: the big single sources are Ashfall locations on
  `LEVEL_SCALE` (1, 2, 3, 4, 6), which pay 6x their level 1 figure at level 5. They were sized
  against the curves. Several pass 100 on their own:
  - Orrery: 108 research
  - Lamplight Depot: 132 travel
  - Hauler Yard: 120 vehicle parts
  - Blacksite and Combine Armories: 120 refits each
  - Bone Market and Rendering Shed: 72 refund each
