# Wiring audit

Wiring audit: what each attribute, label, tag and stacking bonus actually does. One dated section per pass.

## Officer attributes and perks (2026-10-01)

### How this pass measured

- **Fold probe.** A throwaway script (in the gitignored `apps/server/.tmp/`, deleted after the pass) built an in-memory base per case and read `crewEffectsFor` and `standingEffectsFor` from `apps/server/src/crew/standing.ts`: an officer with the attribute at 40 (or carrying the perk) seated, the same officer on the bench, the same officer injured, and a second copy of the attribute or perk on a second officer. Every attribute and every one of the 180 perks was run.
- **Consumer read.** Each channel was followed to the code that spends it, and the label was checked against that code: percent or points, taken off a price or a clock, added to or multiplied with the neighbouring sources, and where a ceiling cuts it off.
- **Who counts.** Only seated officers who are not injured enter the room (`officerIsWorking` in `crewRoomFor`). The bench and the injured put nothing in: no rating, no perk, no lift on anybody else. The probe found no exception across the 35 attributes and 180 perks.
- **How people stack.** Attributes are best-of per attribute after the chair discount (`seatedRating`: rating x chair share x seat uplift, share 100/75/50/25% by tag, uplift up to +16% by fit), and the attributes that share a channel are then summed. Perks sum across the room, copies included. The crew fold, the ground, the Lab, the faction table and the crew's rank are all added (`combineEffects`, `mergeCrewEffects`), never multiplied. The one exception this pass found, resource yield, was made additive (D4).

### Decisions, resolved (maintainer rulings, 2026-10-01)

- **D1. Ceilings are curves now.** Held-ground toughness is `heldDefense` (`battle/effects.ts`): in full to 55, then `softCap` toward 85, never reached. Cohesion is `cohesionWidening` (`battle/engine.ts`): in full to +45%, then toward +65%. Measured: defence 50 stays 50, the old ceiling 65 becomes 63.5, 100 becomes 78.3 (was 65); cohesion 50 becomes 49.4, 100 becomes 63.7 (was 50). A 0 to 300 sweep of each is strictly increasing (`soft-ceilings.test.ts`). The defence, Gate, whole-district and cohesion cards end in "(tapers, no hard stop)".
- **D2. Wages stay agreed at signing.** No mechanic change. Every wage-discount perk reads `off wages agreed at signing while they are working`, every Lab rung `off every wage agreed at signing from now on`, and the channel `Off wages agreed at signing`.
- **D3. Short Way is road only.** `launchMission` spends it on the two travel legs and never on the job's own clock; the send dialog matches. Held by `missions.test.ts` (same job clock led and unled, shorter road led) and the declared-fight march tests in `officer-in-battle.test.ts` and `rail-column.test.ts`.
- **D4. Yield adds, Cap Counter counts job caps, and jobs pay more caps.** `productionRates` pays `1 + (production% + yield%) / 100`. Cap Counter is a new `mission_caps` kind at +8%, applied to a job's caps at the return and quoted on the board. Jobs move a share of their non-caps worth into caps (`MISSION_CAPS_TILT`: 10% of plain work, 25% of fights) at market value. Sim over 40 days of every board at levels 3, 8, 15, 25 and 40 (5,200 plain jobs, 2,600 fights): plain work 1,120.7 worth before and 1,120.6 after, caps 259.8 to 345.9 a job (23.2% to 30.9%), weight 1,144 to 1,142 kg; fights 1,317.9 to 1,317.7 worth, caps 311.5 to 563.1 (23.6% to 42.7%), weight 1,371 to 1,358 kg. Feats: the only caps ladder (`caps_1` to `caps_10`, `resources_earned`) steps by about x2.6 a rung, so jobs paying 1.3 to 1.8 times the caps moves each rung by less than one step; targets left as they are. No feat measured the caps yield, and the feat and pacing suites pass unchanged.
- **D5. `snipers_eye`.** Renamed from `sniper\u2019s_eye`. A saved officer or Overseer holding the old id loses that one perk on read and nothing else; the repair is `knownCommanders` in `db/repos/bases.ts` and `knownPerks` in `db/repos/overseers.ts`, already held by `salvage.test.ts`.
- **Not verified:** whether a district's production window is settled up to the moment a fight injures an officer, or priced whole at the post-injury crew. `crewChangesDue` cuts at recoveries only.

## Research rungs (2026-10-01)

The 160 rungs on the sixteen tracks other than the Master of Whispers'. The Consigliere's track left the game today; the Master of Whispers' had its own pass.

### How this pass measured

- **Fold and reach, every rung.** A throwaway probe (scratchpad, not committed) finished each rung alone on an in-memory crew and diffed three folds against the same crew without it: `researchEffects`, `standingEffectsFor` and `crewEffectsFor`. Every rung moved its channel by exactly its printed number and nothing else, identically on all three. That check is now a test: `apps/server/src/research/rung-reach.test.ts` walks the 162 rungs that carry a bonus (the 160 here and two of the Whispers') through both server folds. Dropping the Lab out of either fold fails all 162.
- **Consumer, every rung.** A second probe called the code that spends each channel on a crew of four seated officers at 50 in every attribute, level 20, every structure at 6 and the Nexus at 12: `buildQuotesFor` and `buildClocksFor`, `minutesFor` on the Lab clock, `trainingRatesFor` into `trainingCost`, `productionRatesFor`, `storeCeilingsOf`, `districtUnitSlots`, `recruitSlotsFor`, `committedWage`, `ledgerFor`, `discountedCaps`, `effectiveStats` for a unit of the right tier or id, `cohesionWidening`, `casualtyRecoveryShare`, `roadMinutes`, `hastenedMinutes`, and for the chair rungs `officerSheetBonusFor`, `officerBattleStats`, `officerTargetShare` and `leadingAs` with the track's chair, another chair and no chair. Where a consumer needs a whole fight or route (Gate, whole district, allies, loot, spoils, refund, infamy, experience, spy points, the switches, the slot grants) the row says "consumer read": the fold is measured and the spending site was read. Those consumers are the ones the officer pass above measured per kind; a rung lands in the same fold a perk does, so it reaches them the same way.
- **Why a printed cut measures smaller.** Every source of a channel is added before it is spent, so a rung lands on top of whatever the crew already has. On a divisor (build, research, training clocks) a -4% card on a crew already at +20% measures -3.71%; on a discount a -15% card on a crew already at 14% off measures -17.4%. Both are the additive rule in `combineEffects`, not a wrong number.
- **When it stops.** A finished rung is the crew's for good: it sits in `base.research.technologies`, the track officer leaving or being hurt does not take it away, and the cards say no more than that. Only three things on these tracks name a chair, and each needs that chair seated and working: the chair rungs of the Field Commander and the Raid Boss (paid only when that chair leads; a benched officer has no chair, an injured one cannot lead), the Right Hand's standing orders (`automations/runners.ts` stalls with nobody fit in the chair, the route refuses to enable one), and the Reimagining bench (needs a working Head of Research).
- **A Lab reset.** There is none in the game. Nothing removes a finished rung except `knownResearch` in `db/repos/bases.ts`, which drops an id the catalogue no longer has on read (the Consigliere's, from today). The admin console only adds rungs, and the Lab building's level gates starting a rung, never one that is finished.
- **Ceilings.** Every unit channel these rungs pay is either open (offense, vitality, tier and kind terms, stims, taunt) or on a curve (defence, Gate, whole district, cohesion, medic points), apart from the 0..100 ratings (armour, morale, speed, stealth), which are the 2026-09-15 ruling. The hard ceilings that do bind are on clocks and prices, not units: build cost 60, training cost 50, wages 50, market 45, vehicles 60, training time 60, road 60, job clock 50. The officer lifts sit inside `MAX_OFFICER_LIFT` (10 an attribute, all sources together), so a Lab people rung adds nothing to an attribute other sources have already filled.

## Building levels and modification cards (2026-10-01)

What each structure's level pays, and all 91 Scrapyard cards that bolt onto a structure, each in its home and in every other structure it fits.

### How this pass measured

- **Levels.** A throwaway probe (scratchpad, not committed) stood one structure at a time at levels 1, 10 and its ceiling on an otherwise empty district and read the server's consumers: `productionRates`, `buildQuotesFor` and `buildClocksFor` (`district/build.ts`), `storeCeilingsOf`, `districtUnitSlots`, `ledgerFor`, `standingEffectsFor` (`gatePercent`, `intelResistancePercent`, `lootCapacityPercent`), `trainingRatesFor` into `trainingSeconds` and `trainingCost`, `minutesFor` on a 130 minute rung, and `awardPlayerXp`. The Generator, Garage, Infirmary and Scrapyard figures were read off `buildingBuildSeconds`, `vehicleBuildSeconds`, `casualtyRecoveryShare` and `scrapyardDiscountPercent`.
- **Cards.** The same probe fitted each card alone into each structure it fits (321 card and structure pairs) on a district with every structure at its ceiling and the Quarters at 10, so the Quarters still has a next level to quote, and diffed every consumer against the bare district. Every pair moved its own channel and nothing else, by exactly its printed number where the channel is a sum. No pair paid nothing. Every card paid the same in every structure it fits, apart from production cards, which by design pay the output of the structure they sit in.
- **Engine.** A card on the Gate's term was followed into a fight at the crew's gate: `battle/gate-defence.test.ts` now holds that an armour card bolted into the Quarters reaches the engine's `gatePercent` once at its printed 26, and leaves `defensePercent` alone.
- **Gates on a card.** A card's four gates (structure level, crew level, notoriety, a chair at a mark) are checked once, when it is bolted in (`boltInRefusal`). The chair is read through `officerFitReader().markFor`, which only sees officers seated and out of bed: measured on Pay Office, the bench, an injured Head of Finance and an empty chair all refuse with "Nobody is sitting as Head of Finance", and a seated one clears. Once fitted, the card keeps paying with the chair empty (book 980 to 1,050 caps either way) and with its structure lowered below the bracket (Nexus 3: 413 against 385 bare), which is the 2026-09-29 ruling for a lowered structure and the rule a finished research rung follows. The four `faction_xp_percent` cards are all BASIC, so the Right Hand that `OFFICER_FOR_EFFECT` now names for them is asked by none of them.
- **When it stops.** Nothing in the game demolishes a structure. The one way a level is lost is a Wall Breaker taking the home Gate down a level (`battle/wall-breaker.ts`), and every Gate figure is read off the live level, so it falls with it. A home district cannot be lost, so "the district lost" does not apply to a structure; it applies to the captured gate, which was not in this pass apart from the spy fix below.
- **Ceilings.** The reductions a card pays are summed per channel and cut at 70 (`MAX_EFFECT_REDUCTION`). Defence is the only unit-facing channel a structure pays, and it goes through the held-ground curve (full to 55, tapers toward 85). The other ceilings that bind are on clocks and prices: Gauntlet 40 and training speed 60, Greenhouse 30 and the supplies cut 40, the yard discount 30, the crew's build discount 60. Training speed and the supplies cut taper since 2026-10-01 (B3, resolved).
